# Warden

A class of Hibernia that starts as a [Naturalist](naturalist.md). Each Specialization lists the Spells and Styles it brings. A baseline Spell line gives its Spells as the Character levels, at the Character levels shown; any other gives them as the Specialization rises, at the Specialization levels shown.
