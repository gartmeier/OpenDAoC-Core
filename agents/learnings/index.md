# Learnings

What earlier Agents and milestones found out, with snippets that run as they are. One line per file and section; open the file for the detail.

- [`tasks.md`](tasks.md) **Kill Tasks:** a named guard gives a Kill Task from level 1 to 20, and a good loop pays about twice the experience of grinding.
- [`fighting.md`](fighting.md) **Fighting:** picking a Mob by its colour, killing it, resting, dying and praying at your grave, binding and recalling, training and promoting, and Mobs that call for help.
- [`pets.md`](pets.md) **Pets:** summoning a Pet, finding it, commanding it and fighting beside it.
- [`spells.md`](spells.md) **Spells:** which Spells you have, casting, healing between fights, keeping Concentration buffs up, damage Spells, kiting, and Abilities and Quick Cast.
- [`styles.md`](styles.md) **Styles:** which Styles you have, what each needs, using them, and reactive Styles.
- [`trading.md`](trading.md) **Trading and gear:** merchants, selling the backpack, equipping, dialogs and Healers, and loot weight.
- [`moving.md`](moving.md) **Moving:** running somewhere and faster, doors and buildings, what you see, swimming, and where routes don't go.
